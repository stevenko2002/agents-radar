## 3. Trend Signal Analysis

Today's hot list reveals three converging signals. First, **agent memory and context management** is the breakout theme — `claude-mem` (+534), `agency-agents` (+687), and `headroom` (20–95% token compression) all address the same pain point: agents forget and waste tokens. The community is shifting from "build an agent" to "make an agent that remembers and stays cheap." Second, **multi-harness orchestration** is emerging as a distinct category. `pstack-claude` and `ECC` both target the fragmentation problem — developers now juggle Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Gemini, and Copilot, and tooling that translates primitives across them is filling a real gap. Third, **vertical agentic applications** (video production in `OpenMontage`, CAD in `text-to-cad`, trading in `Vibe-Trading`, video gen in `MoneyPrinterTurbo`) show that the "agent" label is escaping pure coding use-cases and entering creative/workflow domains. The explosive growth of zero-API-fee agents (`Agent-Reach`) also reflects cost-conscious post-2025 developer sentiment. No major new model release is driving today's list — the momentum is all **infrastructure, memory, and application-layer tooling** around existing models.
