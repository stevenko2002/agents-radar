The dominant trend today is the **explosive growth of AI-powered developer tooling and agent infrastructure**. Projects like **TencentDB-Agent-Memory**, **claude-mem**, **mem0**, and **cognee** all address the same fundamental problem: how to give AI agents persistent, cross-session context. This reflects the industry's recognition that stateless agents are insufficient for real-world productivity.
