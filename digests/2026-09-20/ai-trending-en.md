## 3. Trend Signal Analysis

The dominant narrative today is the **commoditization of agent infrastructure**. The convergence of `claude-code` going open source, `security-audit-skill` hitting `+3,162` stars, and `agent-skills` from both Cloudflare and Addy Osmani reveals that the community is no longer asking *whether* AI agents will be core to development — it's asking *how to build, share, and audit the skills that power them*. This mirrors the "Lego-ification" of the AI stack: instead of monolithic frameworks, we're seeing a proliferation of composable, verifiable skill modules that any agent can plug into.

A second, quieter signal is the rise of **edge and computer-use AI**. `trycua/cua` (`+1,124`) and `cactus-compute/needle` (`+207`) address two under-served frontiers: training/evaluating agents in real desktop environments, and running capable models on microcontrollers and phones. This suggests the community is anticipating a post-cloud AI paradigm where inference happens locally and agents interact with the physical desktop, not just APIs.

Finally, the steady presence of RAG infrastructure (`ragflow`, `dify`, `claude-mem`, `docling`) indicates that **context and memory** remain the hardest unsolved problems — and the most fertile open-source opportunities. The trending repos today are less about model novelty and more about the plumbing that makes agents actually useful in production.
