   Key contribution: Uncovers a widespread, previously overlooked floating-point underflow failure mode in ALiBi positional encodings that silently blinds large portions of attention heads, with direct implications for the reliability of all models trained with this popular encoding scheme.

   Authors: Taekyung Heo et al.  
   Key contribution: Proposes a lightweight closed-form linear mapping to transfer KV caches between models of different sizes in the same family, eliminating redundant prefill computation for cost-quality cascading, mid-conversation model switching, and routing in production deployments.

   Authors: Alberto Acedo  
   Key contribution: Introduces Omega-S, a data-free, 3-line penalty computed directly from weight matrices to mitigate catastrophic forgetting during LLM fine-tuning, requiring no access to prior task data or stored base model weights for practical, resource-constrained fine-tuning workflows.
